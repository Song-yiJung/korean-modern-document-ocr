佛國寺多寶塔 a Tahōtō (Prabhūtaratna* pagoda)
at Bukkoku-ji.
慶州郡內東面進峴里一五番 社寺地
Shingen-ri, Nai tō-men, Keishū-gun, N. Keishō Prov
Bukkoku-ji.
RB. Reg 8/27/34.
no descr. no photo.

* This kind of pagoda is supposed to represent
that in which Prabhūtaratna Buddha appeared
when Śākyamuni Buddha was preaching the
Saddharmapuṇḍarīka sūtra (Lotus of the Good Law),
It is usually of only one story and in
Japan at least usually has a
dome as well as a roof. This one is
of stone. Cf. CRZF 4.408 +
Ryūkoku Daigaku Bukkyō Daijiten
5, 3956.
